Regional & currency settings

Choose a standard currency or enter your own ISO currency code and display symbol. This setting is used across inventory prices, sales, quotations, invoices, reports and PDFs.

New sales and quotations use the selected currency; previous documents retain the currency in which they were created. Changing currency does not apply exchange rates. When decimal precision changes, product prices are rescaled to preserve their numeric amount; review and reprice them for the new currency before issuing documents. For currencies normally displayed without decimals (such as JPY or KRW), select 0 decimal places. For three-decimal currencies such as BHD, KWD and OMR, select 3 decimal places.
